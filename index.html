About

I am an applied and ethical AI researcher focusing on conversational systems, human–computer interaction, and machine learning. My work investigates trustworthy AI and ethical conversational systems for decision-making across information-intensive domains.

Research Interests

  • Conversational AI & Information Retrieval
  • Ethical AI, Trust, and Governance
  • Applied AI & Human–AI Interaction
  • NLP for socio-technical problems

Research Labs

I direct two labs that approach the same problem from opposite ends. ICANN builds and probes conversational systems themselves; PAVE studies the people who use them and the values that shape them. Visit the lab website →

ICANN Lab

Intelligent Conversational Agents and Neural Networks

Dialogue models, retrieval, evaluation infrastructure, and computational social science at scale.

Visit the lab →

PAVE Lab

People, AI, Values & Evaluation

Human-AI interaction, responsible AI, evaluation and trust, and applied AI in public-interest settings.

Visit the lab →
