About

I am an applied and ethical AI researcher working on conversational systems, human-computer interaction, and machine learning. My work investigates trustworthy AI and ethical conversational systems for decision-making across information-intensive domains.

I hold a PhD in Communication, Information, and Media from Rutgers University, where my dissertation on spoken conversational search systems was runner-up for the iSchools Doctoral Dissertation Award. Before joining the University of South Carolina I spent six years at San José State University, where I co-led the Human-Centered AI certificate and minor and served as an AI Faculty Fellow.

02

Research interests

  1. 01

    Conversational AI and information retrieval

    Spoken conversational search, clarification and mixed-initiative dialogue, speech acts, and how a conversational session behaves when there is no screen to fall back on.

  2. 02

    Ethical AI, trust, and governance

    Bias and stereotype audits of model behavior, calibrated trust, accountability, and governance frameworks for institutions adopting AI-mediated services.

  3. 03

    Applied AI and human-AI interaction

    How people communicate, seek information, and make decisions with AI in libraries, archives, health information, education, and front-line work.

  4. 04

    NLP for socio-technical problems

    Misinformation, dehumanization, sentiment and stance at scale, and language processing for code-mixed and low-resource text.
